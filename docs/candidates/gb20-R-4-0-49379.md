**Verdict:** `known population / processing: slow dimming` (`hypothesis` for the cause). The feature is a smooth
~5 % dimming of a red giant over ~270 d. Semi-regular or long-period red-giant variability, or a slow
difference-imaging residual, would produce it. No in-domain W3 model fits it as well as an ordinary dip. Status:
**explained; not a candidate.** Nothing goes to the owner or outside the repository.

**Rule (folded into the chain and the w3-survey skill):** a far-field exotic fit (u₀ ≫ 1) with cancelling giant
f_s and f_b can mimic any smooth dip. Require the fit domain (u₀ < 2) and a physical source flux (f_s ≤ 3 × the
reference flux), and test a smooth Gaussian dip. Re-run injections after the change: the limits in
docs/exotic_limits.md include both tests.
