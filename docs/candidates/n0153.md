**Verdict:** `inconclusive: needs a third epoch (or a spectrum)`. The 2022→2025 brightening of `n0153` is real
at the data level. Persistence, calibration, pipeline version, edges, cosmic rays, deblending, solar-system
objects and lensing do not explain it. A variable star is disfavoured but not excluded. The most likely explanation is
ordinary: a nuclear variable or transient in a compact z ≈ 2 galaxy (AGN variability, or a supernova or TDE near the nucleus). This is a
`hypothesis`. Two epochs cannot tell these apart, and nothing here points beyond known populations. Status: open
transient candidate. It is not a lensing anomaly.

**What would change the plan:** a third epoch that shows a return to the 2022 flux (a transient), or continued
stochastic variability (an AGN). A NIRSpec or NIRISS spectrum would show broad lines (AGN) or SN features. Without
new JWST epochs, archival tests remain: the DJA per-epoch mosaics (the colour of the varying component, and a
less epoch-mixed SED for the star test) and archival X-ray or radio counterparts (AGN).
