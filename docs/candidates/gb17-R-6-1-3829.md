### The light curve (`observed`)

The flux is near 0 counts in 2006–2008. In 2009 it starts varying with ~30-day minima. In 2010–2011 the mean is
lowered by ~3,000 counts and deep minima of −8,000 … −15,000 counts recur. The level is raised (~+1,500) in 2013,
then near 0 in 2014. No caustic spikes are sampled.
