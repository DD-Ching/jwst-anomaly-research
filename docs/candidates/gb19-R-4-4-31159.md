**Verdict:** `known population / processing: one-sided step` (`hypothesis` for the cause). The feature is a
season-boundary level change with a slow partial recovery that never returns to the earlier baseline. Possible
causes are a local reference-image or blending change for this star, or intrinsic variability. A chip-wide
calibration change is ruled out above. It is not an umbra crossing: an umbra crossing has baseline on both sides.
Status: **explained; not a candidate.** Nothing goes to the owner or outside the repository.

**Rule (folded into the chain and the w3-survey skill):** an exotic feature must be bracketed by baseline on both
sides; a one-sided step or secular change is not an umbra crossing. The chain requires `feature_bracketed`
(≥ 20 baseline epochs on each side, ASSUMPTION) and tests a step-plus-ramp model with free t_s. The injections
were re-run after the change, so the limits include both tests (events near the data edges pay for it).
