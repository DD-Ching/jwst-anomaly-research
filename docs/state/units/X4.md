# X4: wave-optics GW lensing by negative-mass compact objects (Ellis wormholes as a separate model)

Authorized under the trial (D-083) by S0-2026-10-15 (`docs/state/units/S0-2026-10-15.md`, direction A).
Rules: trial-2026-10-13.2.

## Premises
- **Hypothesis** (not a result): negative-mass compact objects (n = 1, ε < 0) of 10–10⁴ M☉ exist along the line
  of sight to some LVK binaries. Ellis wormholes (zero mass, n = 2) are a separate lens model, added in S1. They
  are not assumed to act like a negative point mass.
- **Signature** [derived, S0]: the amplification factor of a repulsive point lens is a low-pass filter. For a
  source behind the lens (y < 2), high frequencies are suppressed, the wave-optics W3 dip. Positive lenses
  amplify. The closed form, its checks and its distinguishability against a positive point mass are in S0 A.
- **Ordinary models to beat:**
  - positive lenses: point mass, SIS, embedded, binary;
  - unlensed signals with free intrinsic parameters: masses, spins, precession, higher modes, eccentricity;
  - calibration;
  - noise transients.
- **Labels:** S0's results are derived and simulated. No strain has been analysed. GW231123 is a published
  outlier under positive-lens models, not a candidate of ours.

## S1 (started 2026-10-15; owner request, ≤ 4 h)
Question: does a negative-mass point lens leave a residual after free ordinary binary parameters? The Ellis n = 2
lens is deferred: it needs its own throat and time scales, and is not mapped onto a point-lens mass.

### Frozen before computing (2026-10-15)
- **Waveform:** IMRPhenomXAS (aligned spins, dominant (2, |2|) mode only), lalsimulation 6.2.1 from lalsuite
  7.7.1 (PyPI). It is installed in a separate scratch environment, not as a project dependency.
  - **Not covered:** precession, higher modes, eccentricity, waveform-model systematics.
- **Noise:** aLIGO design sensitivity, `lalsimulation.SimNoisePSDaLIGODesignSensitivityP1200087`
  (LIGO-P1200087), single detector.
- **Band:** 20–1,024 Hz, Δf = 0.25 Hz.
- **Injections** (detector frame): total mass 30, 60 and 200 M☉; mass ratio m1/m2 = 1.5; aligned spins
  χ1z = χ2z = 0.2.
- **Lenses:** negative point lens with M_Lz ∈ {100, 300, 1,000, 3,000} M☉ and y ∈ {0.3, 1.0, 1.5}. That is 12 × 3 =
  36 cases. The S0 closed form F_s(w, y) is evaluated exactly with mpmath on every frequency.
- **Ordinary models,** each with amplitude, arrival time and phase maximised analytically:
  - **U (unlensed):** chirp mass free (bounded at ± 30 % of the injection's), mass ratio 1–8, χ1z and χ2z in
    [−0.99, 0.99].
  - **P (positive point lens):** the same as U, plus M_Lz in [1, 10⁴] M☉ and y in [0.01, 5].
- **Optimiser:**
  - U: Nelder–Mead on (ln M_c, q, χ1, χ2), started from the injection, from the best of a coarse chirp-mass and
    mass-ratio scan, and from two random points.
  - P: for each node of a 10 × 10 grid in (ln M_Lz, y), Nelder–Mead on the intrinsic parameters, started from
    the U best fit; then a local Nelder–Mead over all six parameters from the best three nodes.
  - Fitting factor FF = the best overlap.
- **Tool validation before the 36 cases:**
  - an unlensed injection fitted with U must give FF ≥ 0.999;
  - a positive-lens injection fitted with P must give FF ≥ 0.995.
- **Robustness:** every case that passes the screening threshold is re-fitted from three different starts and
  with widened bounds (chirp mass ± 60 %). It counts only if the residual persists.
- **Screening threshold** (unchanged, **a screening threshold for the next stage**, not a 3σ detection or a
  calibrated false-alarm rate):
  - ρ_req = 3/√(1 − FF²), for the observed (lensed) SNR.
  - Pass if ρ_req ≤ 30 against both U and P, and if ρ_req ≤ 100 × (lensed/unlensed SNR ratio), i.e. an intrinsic
    SNR ≤ 100.
  - The intrinsic SNR needed is reported as well.
- **Outcome rules:**
  - Cases that do not converge are **undetermined**.
  - If every case is absorbed, this model and case set pause; the GW direction is not declared infeasible.
  - No real event is analysed in S1.

**Results:**
- **Main run (36 cases, all converged, none undetermined).** 19 pass and 17 do not.
  - **Too demagnified** (13): M_Lz ≥ 1,000 M☉ at y = 0.3; M_Lz = 3,000 M☉ at every y; and the 200 M☉ binary
    behind 1,000 M☉ at y ≤ 1. Large residuals, but they need an intrinsic SNR of 124 to ~5 × 10⁵.
  - **Absorbed by a positive point lens** (4): the 200 M☉ binary behind 100 M☉ at every y, and behind 300 M☉ at
    y = 1.5. FF_P 0.9977–0.9996, ρ_req 44–107.
- **Frozen refinement (q ≤ 8):** all 19 still pass.
  - FF_P rises by ≤ 0.009.
  - The 6-parameter Nelder–Mead used only 80 evaluations per start. It beat the grid in 9 of 19 cases and is
    under-converged.
  - The verdicts therefore use `summary`, the best fit over all stages. **q ≤ 8:** 19 pass; ρ_req (observed)
    4.4–20.1; intrinsic SNR needed 17–96.
- **Mass ratio up to 15** (the best fit over all stages, the most conservative result on disk): **18 of 19 still
  pass.**
  - Best ordinary FF: 0.63–0.98 (unlensed) and 0.75–0.99 (positive lens).
  - ρ_req (observed) 4.5–20.1; intrinsic SNR needed 20–95.
  - The case that drops out, 60 M☉ behind 1,000 M☉ at y = 1, is still separable (ρ_req 4.8) but needs intrinsic
    SNR 101 > 100.
  - Most U and P fits end at q ≈ 15, the new bound. The q ≤ 15 result is **bound-conditional**, not converged in
    q. **Extreme mass ratios are the direction in which ordinary
    binaries absorb part of the signature**, and that is where a dominant-mode-only waveform is least
    trustworthy. Higher modes and precession must be included before any real-data use.
- **Waveform family:** IMRPhenomD fits give FFs within 0.0065 of the IMRPhenomXAS fits for the three key cases,
  and all three still pass.
  - **Correction:** the first cross-check (commit df555b9) silently used IMRPhenomXAS for both. Its "≤ 0.0002"
    figure was wrong; found by review and rerun.
- **Why the residual survives.** Behind a negative-mass lens the high frequencies are suppressed and the lens adds
  a frequency-dependent phase. Changing masses or spins shifts the whole signal's frequency content, and a
  positive point lens amplifies rather than suppresses. Neither reproduces both the amplitude cut and the phase.

**Disclosed deviations and limits:**
- The main run's P refinement (5 × 5 grid, intrinsic only) was not the frozen one. The frozen 6-parameter
  refinement ran afterwards on every passing case, and the verdicts above use it. Failing cases were not
  refined, because a better ordinary fit can only lower a residual, never raise one.
- Overlaps use pad 4 (0.24 ms lags) in the main run, which biases FF low by ≤ 3e-3 at 30 M☉. The refine stages
  use pad 16.
- Many fits sit on bounds: q at 8 (or 15), |χ| at 0.99, or the chirp-mass limit.
- **Geometric optics** replaces the exact lens factor where mpmath fails (very large w), and from the refine stage
  on wherever w > 300 and w·y² > 6,000. There it agrees to ≤ 1e-4; elsewhere at w ≥ 150 to ≤ 1e-3 for
  y ≥ 1.5 (8.7e-3 at y = 0.3, w = 300). It touches only positive-lens factors at large M_Lz; the injections'
  negative-lens factors are exact. The fallback counters in the JSON read 0 because arrays came from the disk
  cache.
- **Not covered:**
  - precession, higher modes (important at high q), eccentricity, waveform systematics beyond two aligned-spin
    families;
  - other ordinary lenses (SIS, embedded or binary microlenses, as used for GW231123);
  - a detector network and calibration errors (one aLIGO design-curve detector);
  - real noise and glitches;
  - priors and selection;
  - the abundance of such lenses (hypothetical).
  - The fitting factors are noise-free expectations.

### Frozen before computing (2026-10-15)
- **Representative cases** (exploratory, chosen from S1 results, not random):
  - R1, large difference: 30 M☉ behind a 300 M☉ lens, y 0.3 (S1 ρ_req 5.7);
  - R2, closest to the threshold among the passing cases: 60 M☉, 100 M☉, y 1.5 (ρ_req 20.1);
  - R3, 200 M☉ source: 200 M☉, 300 M☉, y 1.0 (ρ_req 18.2);
  - R4, an absorbed control: 200 M☉, 100 M☉, y 1.0 (FF_P 0.9987, ρ_req 58).
- **Step A, re-score.** Every saved best-fit parameter set for these cases (all S1 stages) is re-evaluated on the
  same frequency grid with a finer time maximisation (pad 64, lag step 0.06 ms).
- **Step B, multi-start fits with convergence checks.**
  - Starts: all saved best solutions, plus random starts (U: 12; P: 3).
  - Nelder–Mead with up to 400 evaluations per run, restarted from its end point with a fresh simplex until a
    restart improves 1 − FF by < 1e-5 (at most 4 restarts).
  - Saved per run: stop reason, evaluations, improvement history, bound contacts (within 1 % of a bound).
  - Domains: U and P with q ≤ 15 as in S1, plus a diagnostic U with q ≤ 50, which explores the domain and does
    not change the verdict.
- **Step C, fit-error validation.**
  - Ordinary signals (the best-fit U and P solutions of each case) are injected and fitted with the same
    procedure, started away from the truth.
  - The fit error ε = 1 − FF on these must be ≤ 0.1 × (1 − FF_case) for that model; otherwise the case is
    **undetermined**.
- **Verdict per case (numerical stage):**
  - **Residual persists in the tested domain** if ρ_req ≤ 30 against U and P, the best solution is reached by ≥ 2
    different starts within 2e-4 in FF, the last restart improves by < 1e-5, and ε passes. Any best-fit
    parameter on a bound makes it **bound-conditional**.
  - **Absorbed** if ρ_req > 30.
  - **Undetermined** otherwise.
- **Step D (only after the numerical stage): precession and higher modes**, IMRPhenomXPHM.
  - Cost measured first (2.8–15 ms per waveform).
  - **Target:** IMRPhenomXPHM, q 1.5, aligned spins 0.2, inclination 60°, reference phase 0, detector response
    h₊ (polarisation angle 0), times F_neg.
  - **Ordinary templates:**
    - chirp mass, q ≤ 15, both spin vectors (Cartesian, |χ| ≤ 0.99), inclination, reference phase;
    - polarisation angle ψ through h = cos 2ψ h₊ + sin 2ψ h×;
    - arrival time and amplitude maximised;
    - **no free overall phase:** the real part of the correlation is maximised, because with higher modes a
      constant phase rotation is not a physical change.
  - **P with higher modes:** the waveform is fitted with the lens fixed at the best numerical-stage P lens, then
    the lens is polished with the waveform fixed; two alternations.
  - Same threshold. If the residual disappears, record which degrees of freedom absorbed it (inclination,
    in-plane spins, q).
- **Threshold unchanged** (ρ_req ≤ 30 for the observed SNR and intrinsic SNR ≤ 100), a screening threshold for the
  next stage. If the budget runs out, record the gap; no forced verdict.

  production `objective` with the exact lens. ε = 0, 3.3e-16, 0 and 1.1e-16 for V5, V6, V7 and V9; every truth
  lies inside the box.
- **Grid floor:** with the true intrinsic parameters, the best of the 1,271 cached lens nodes leaves ε 1.3e-3
  (V5) and 9.2e-4 (V9). Both are above the 5e-4 rule.
  - The best nodes sit on the lens degeneracy (2,512 M☉ / 0.079 and 3,162 M☉ / 0.098), not at the truth. The
    nearest nodes are worse (ε 3.0e-2 and 9.1e-3).
  - **So the grid alone cannot pass validation.** It can only locate the region.
- **Continuous refinement works from there:** alternating an exact-lens 2-D Nelder–Mead (40 evaluations) with the
  intrinsic Nelder–Mead restarts. From (true intrinsics, best node) it reaches ε 4.4e-6 (V5; 7 rounds, 417 s) and
  5.7e-6 (V9; 4 rounds, 240 s), walking back along the degeneracy to 1,017 M☉ / 0.197 and 1,432 M☉ / 0.215.

### Frozen before the search (2026-10-15)
- **Target and score:** as in S1d (XPHM; polarisation and amplitude maximised analytically; parameter box
  unchanged). **Change from S1d:** pass and fail use the final score with the exact lens factor at pad 64.
- **U:** DE over 10 parameters.
  - Latin-hypercube initialisation, so the population is exactly 120 (Sobol rounds it up), at most 600
    generations, mutation (0.5, 1), recombination 0.7, tol 1e-4.
  - Then Nelder–Mead restarts until a restart gains < 1e-5 (at most 8).
- **P (joint):** one DE over 12 parameters, the 10 intrinsic ones plus ln M_Lz ∈ [0, ln 10⁴] and ln y ∈ [ln 0.01,
  ln 5]. The lens is snapped to the nearest cached grid node in (ln M_Lz, ln y).
  - Population exactly 144, at most 400 generations, the same DE settings.
  - Then **continuous refinement** with exact lens factors: the exact-lens 2-D Nelder–Mead alternated with the
    intrinsic restarts, until a round gains < 1e-5 (at most 10 rounds).
- **Parallelism:** each fit runs as one process (DE workers = 1), with 4 fits at a time on the 4 cores.
- **Saved per fit:** the best value every 10 generations, evaluations, the DE stop message and final population
  spread, each refinement round's value and gain, and bound contacts. Generation caps are resource settings,
  not evidence of convergence.
- **Validation:** V6, V7 (U) and V5, V9 (P), seeds 11 and 12, the same formal procedure. **Pass:** every injection
  and seed has ε ≤ 5e-4, and ε ≤ 0.1 × (1 − FF_case) for that model.
  - Failed seeds are kept, the threshold is not lowered, and no near-truth starts are used.
  - These injections were used during development, so a pass supports tool calibration only. It is not an
    independent test of generalisation.
- **R1 (only after a pass):**
  - Fixed S1c family-D target. U and P with seeds 21, 22 and 23.
  - The saved R1 family-D solutions (S1c) are re-scored on the same target, and the best one for each model is
    added to that model's initial DE population.
  - The verdict uses the best ordinary fit over all valid searches. Reliability needs the two best seeds within
    2e-4.
  - The attribution of the XAS → XPHM change stays undetermined (not run, by the owner's choice).
- **Outcomes:**
  1. An ordinary solution with ρ_req > 30 → R1 does not pass the screen.
  2. Validation and reliability pass and ρ_req ≤ 30 → this supports a pilot limited to the tested models.
  3. Otherwise undetermined, naming the cause: grid precision, search reliability, model scope or budget.
- A best value is not a strict bound, and agreement between seeds does not prove a global optimum.
- **Budget split:** diagnostics 0.15 h (done); validation about 1.2 h; R1 about 1.2 h; write-up and review about
  0.7 h. At 23:40 UTC results are saved and the round stops.

  - generations 200 → 600;
  - initialisation Sobol → Latin hypercube;
  - population updating deferred (pooled workers) → immediate (one process);

  Which of these mattered is not isolated.
- **The lensed component works in 3 of 4 runs** (ε 2.4e-5 – 1.5e-4).
