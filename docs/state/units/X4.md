# X4: wave-optics GW lensing by negative-mass compact objects (Ellis wormholes as a separate model)

Authorized under the trial (D-083) by S0-2026-10-15 (`docs/state/units/S0-2026-10-15.md`, direction A).
Rules: trial-2026-10-13.2.

## Premises
- **Hypothesis** (not a result): negative-mass compact objects (n = 1, ε < 0) of 10–10⁴ M☉ exist along the line
  of sight to some LVK binaries. Ellis wormholes (zero mass, n = 2) are a separate lens model, added in S1. They
  are not assumed to act like a negative point mass.
- **Signature** [derived, S0]: the amplification factor of a repulsive point lens is a low-pass filter. For a
  source behind the lens (y < 2), high frequencies are suppressed, the wave-optics W3 dip. Positive lenses
  amplify. The closed form, its checks and its distinguishability against a positive point mass are in S0 A.
- **Ordinary models to beat:**
  - positive lenses: point mass, SIS, embedded, binary;
  - unlensed signals with free intrinsic parameters: masses, spins, precession, higher modes, eccentricity;
  - calibration;
  - noise transients.
- **Labels:** S0's results are derived and simulated. No strain has been analysed. GW231123 is a published
  outlier under positive-lens models, not a candidate of ours.

## Stages and gates (written before computing)
**S1. Intrinsic degeneracy** (≤ 4 h, proposed, not started; refined after the review of #233).
- Tools:
  - an IMR waveform model, e.g. IMRPhenomXAS via lalsuite or pycbc, installed from PyPI in a separate
    environment (free; no project dependency until reuse is decided);
  - the S0 F_s(w, y), extended to the Ellis n = 2 lens.
- Work: fitting factors of negative-lens signals against
  - (a) unlensed templates with free chirp mass, mass ratio and aligned spins;
  - (b) positive point-mass lensed templates.
- Positive-lens templates in (b) also get free intrinsic parameters.
- **Configuration list, fixed before computing:**
  - binaries of 30, 60 and 200 M☉ (detector frame; the last GW231123-like);
  - negative lenses with M_Lz ∈ {100, 300, 1,000, 3,000} M☉ and y ∈ {0.3, 1.0, 1.5};
  - 36 cases in all, plus the Ellis n = 2 lens at the same y for 300 and 1,000 M☉.
- **Detectability weighting:** a configuration counts only if the observed SNR ρ_obs = |F|-weighted intrinsic SNR
  can reach 8–30 for an intrinsic SNR ≤ 100. Record the demagnified volume fraction for each.
- **Gate:**
  - **Continue** to S2 if 1 − FF² ≥ 9/ρ² at some ρ ≤ 30 against both (a) and (b), for at least one counted
    configuration with M_Lz ≥ 100 M☉.
  - **Otherwise stop:** the GW route is degenerate, which is recorded as a result.

**S2. GW231123 and the loudest events on GWOSC strain** (≤ 10 h across sessions, proposed).
- Bayesian model comparison of negative lens vs positive lens vs unlensed, with bilby and a relative-binning
  likelihood.
- Calibrated first on ≥ 200 injections of each ordinary model into real noise around the event.
- Frozen priors.
- **Gate:** a negative-lens Bayes factor over the best ordinary model that injections show to be rare under the
  ordinary hypothesis (≤ 1 % false-alarm) is vetted as a needs-human item. Anything less gives a per-event
  constraint.

**S3. Population limit** (≤ 4 h, proposed): from S2's per-event results and published optical-depth models, a
limit on the cosmic density of repulsive compact lenses. Compare with Takahashi & Asada (arXiv:1303.1301; quasar
lensing).

## S1 (started 2026-10-15; owner request, ≤ 4 h)
Question: does a negative-mass point lens leave a residual after free ordinary binary parameters? The Ellis n = 2
lens is deferred: it needs its own throat and time scales, and is not mapped onto a point-lens mass.

### Frozen before computing (2026-10-15)
- **Waveform:** IMRPhenomXAS (aligned spins, dominant (2, |2|) mode only), lalsimulation 6.2.1 from lalsuite
  7.7.1 (PyPI). It is installed in a separate scratch environment, not as a project dependency.
  - **Not covered:** precession, higher modes, eccentricity, waveform-model systematics.
- **Noise:** aLIGO design sensitivity, `lalsimulation.SimNoisePSDaLIGODesignSensitivityP1200087`
  (LIGO-P1200087), single detector.
- **Band:** 20–1,024 Hz, Δf = 0.25 Hz.
- **Injections** (detector frame): total mass 30, 60 and 200 M☉; mass ratio m1/m2 = 1.5; aligned spins
  χ1z = χ2z = 0.2.
- **Lenses:** negative point lens with M_Lz ∈ {100, 300, 1,000, 3,000} M☉ and y ∈ {0.3, 1.0, 1.5}. That is 12 × 3 =
  36 cases. The S0 closed form F_s(w, y) is evaluated exactly with mpmath on every frequency.
- **Ordinary models,** each with amplitude, arrival time and phase maximised analytically:
  - **U (unlensed):** chirp mass free (bounded at ± 30 % of the injection's), mass ratio 1–8, χ1z and χ2z in
    [−0.99, 0.99].
  - **P (positive point lens):** the same as U, plus M_Lz in [1, 10⁴] M☉ and y in [0.01, 5].
- **Optimiser:**
  - U: Nelder–Mead on (ln M_c, q, χ1, χ2), started from the injection, from the best of a coarse chirp-mass and
    mass-ratio scan, and from two random points.
  - P: for each node of a 10 × 10 grid in (ln M_Lz, y), Nelder–Mead on the intrinsic parameters, started from
    the U best fit; then a local Nelder–Mead over all six parameters from the best three nodes.
  - Fitting factor FF = the best overlap.
- **Tool validation before the 36 cases:**
  - an unlensed injection fitted with U must give FF ≥ 0.999;
  - a positive-lens injection fitted with P must give FF ≥ 0.995.
- **Robustness:** every case that passes the screening threshold is re-fitted from three different starts and
  with widened bounds (chirp mass ± 60 %). It counts only if the residual persists.
- **Screening threshold** (unchanged, **a screening threshold for the next stage**, not a 3σ detection or a
  calibrated false-alarm rate):
  - ρ_req = 3/√(1 − FF²), for the observed (lensed) SNR.
  - Pass if ρ_req ≤ 30 against both U and P, and if ρ_req ≤ 100 × (lensed/unlensed SNR ratio), i.e. an intrinsic
    SNR ≤ 100.
  - The intrinsic SNR needed is reported as well.
- **Outcome rules:**
  - Cases that do not converge are **undetermined**.
  - If every case is absorbed, this model and case set pause; the GW direction is not declared infeasible.
  - No real event is analysed in S1.

### S1 results (2026-10-15; `scripts/x4_s1_degeneracy.py`; `results/x4/s1_*.json`)
Labels: injections `simulated`; fitting factors and SNRs `derived`. **No data was analysed; nothing here is
evidence that negative-mass objects exist.** The threshold below is a **screening threshold for the next stage**,
not a 3σ detection and not a calibrated false-alarm rate.

- **Tool validation (passed):**
  - U on unlensed injections: FF 0.99995 (60 M☉) and 0.99986 (200 M☉), started away from the truth.
  - P on an off-grid positive lens (M_Lz 250 M☉, y 0.7): FF 0.9963 (60 M☉) and 0.99986 (200 M☉).
  - The P optimiser's floor at 60 M☉, 1 − FF² ≈ 0.0073, corresponds to ρ_req ≈ 35. A residual against P is
    attributed to physics only when it lies well below that FF.
- **Geometric-optics fallback.** mpmath's ₁F₁ fails to converge at w of several hundred. There the lens factor uses
  geometric optics with the same phase convention, which agrees with the exact form to ≤ 1e-3 at w ≥ 150
  (tested). It was used only on the positive-lens grid at large M_Lz; the injections' negative-lens factors are
  all exact. Lens factors are cached on disk and shared by all stages.
- **36 cases (all converged; none undetermined).** 19 pass the screen and 17 do not.
  - **Not passing, too demagnified** (13 cases): M_Lz ≥ 1,000 M☉ at y = 0.3; M_Lz = 3,000 M☉ at every y; and the
    200 M☉ binary behind 1,000 M☉ at y ≤ 1. Their residuals are large, but the intrinsic SNR needed is 124 to
    ~5 × 10⁵.
  - **Not passing, absorbed** (4 cases): the 200 M☉ binary behind 100 M☉ lenses at every y, and behind 300 M☉ at
    y = 1.5. The positive point lens reaches FF 0.9977–0.9996, so ρ_req = 44–107.
  - **Passing** (observed-SNR ρ_req ≤ 30 against both U and P, intrinsic SNR ≤ 100):

    The table uses the best FF over the main run and all re-fits.
- **Robustness (pre-registered):** every passing case was re-fitted from three new starts with chirp-mass bounds
  of ± 60 %. The best FF moved by ≤ 0.008, and **all 19 still pass**.
- **Waveform-family cross-check:** three key cases fitted with IMRPhenomD templates (injection IMRPhenomXAS) give
  the same FFs to ≤ 0.0002; all three still pass.
- **Why the residual survives.** Behind a negative-mass lens the high frequencies are suppressed and the lens adds
  a frequency-dependent phase. Changing masses or spins shifts the frequency content of the whole signal, and a
  positive point lens amplifies rather than suppresses. Neither reproduces both the amplitude cut and the phase.
- **Not covered (limits):**
  - precession, higher modes, eccentricity, waveform systematics beyond the two aligned-spin families;
  - other ordinary lenses (SIS, embedded or binary microlenses, as used for GW231123);
  - a detector network and calibration errors (a single aLIGO design-curve detector);
  - real noise and glitches;
  - parameter-estimation priors and selection effects;
  - the abundance of such lenses (hypothetical).
  - The fitting factors are noise-free expectations.
- **S1 gate** (as written in this file): continue if 1 − FF² ≥ 9/ρ² at ρ ≤ 30 against U and P, for a counted
  configuration with M_Lz ≥ 100 M☉. **Met** (19 configurations; M_Lz 100–1,000 M☉; binaries 30–200 M☉).

### S2, minimal plan (proposed, NOT started; S1 analyses no real event)
**S2a pilot** (≤ 3 h):
- GW231123_135430, GWOSC strain (H1, L1).
- Maximum-likelihood comparison of three models: unlensed, positive point lens, negative point lens.
- The likelihood is relative-binning or heterodyned, with a precessing higher-mode waveform (IMRPhenomXPHM),
  because GW231123's published fits need high spins.
- Calibration: 50 injections of the best-fit ordinary models into nearby off-source noise give the distribution
  of the statistic under the ordinary hypothesis.
- **Gate** (to be frozen before the strain is read): proceed to a full Bayesian S2 only if the negative-lens
  statistic exceeds the 98th percentile of the injections; otherwise record a per-event constraint.

**S2b** (≤ 7 h, only after S2a): nested sampling with frozen priors, ≥ 200 injections for a ≤ 1 % false-alarm
estimate, and vetting of any outlier as a needs-human item.
