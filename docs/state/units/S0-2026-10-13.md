## A. Wave transmission and echoes of a traversable throat (SV / Ellis–Bronnikov)
1. **Unknown.** How much of a signal at frequency ω and multipole ℓ passes the throat (channel bandwidth;
   propagation). Does a double-peaked SV throat (2M < a < 3M) reflect enough to give ringdown echoes that LVK
   data could limit (existence)?
2. **Ordinary model.** Kerr ringdown with no echoes, plus detector noise and glitches. For bandwidth, a
   free-space aperture of the same radius.
3. **Minimum verification.**
   - Transmission is already published. Do not redo:
     - SV photon and Dirac greybody factors by direct scattering, arXiv:2606.08351;
     - Ellis low-energy transmission, 2010.15023;
     - EM/GW suppression below the Ellis–Bronnikov barrier, 2604.14238;
     - SV quasinormal modes and echoes at the BH–WH transition, 1911.11823.
   - The open piece is mapping LVK echo results onto SV parameters. LVK releases only p-values and Bayes
     factors (2112.06861 Table XIV; 2010.14529), with no reflectivity limit. A mapping needs SV echo templates
     injected into strain data and searched with the LVK method. No public template code exists.
   - Estimated cost: well over the 20 h unit cap (GWOSC strain plus a BayesWave-type search).
4. **Continue / stop.** The theory half reproduces published results. The data half does not fit the budget.
   **Not qualified now.**
   - Reopen if public SV or ECO echo templates, or a reflectivity-level LVK release, appear (the echo files
     are in LIGO DCC P2100456 `ech.tar.gz`, 191 kB, p-values only).

## B. Far-side mass perturbing orbits on our side (Dai & Stojkovic, arXiv:1910.00429)
1. **Unknown.** Does a mass on the other side of a throat at Sgr A* leave a gravitational imprint on S2's orbit
   (existence; a "gravity channel")?
2. **Ordinary model.** Kepler plus Schwarzschild precession, Rømer delay and redshift, plus extended mass or an
   unseen perturber. GRAVITY allows about 1,200 M☉ inside S2's orbit (arXiv:2409.12261).
3. **Minimum verification.**
   - Data exist: CDS J/ApJ/837/30 (145 astrometric epochs and 44 radial velocities), J/ApJ/854/12 and
     J/ApJ/948/94.
   - The derivation, done here [derived, order of magnitude]:
     - A far-side mass m orbiting at r_p of a few to 30 r_g contributes a static monopole. That is exactly
       degenerate with the central mass M.
     - Its time-varying part has a quadrupole-to-monopole acceleration ratio of (r_p/d)². At S2's pericentre
       (d ≈ 2,830 r_g) that is 1e-6 for r_p = 3, 1.3e-5 for 10 and 1.1e-4 for 30 r_g, even for m = M.
     - That part also oscillates on hours. S2's orbital response to it is suppressed by about
       (P_p/P_S2)² ~ 10⁻¹⁰.
     - S2's relative astrometric precision is about 2e-4.
   - Simonetti et al.'s strongest published limits (2007.12184) used HR 6819 and LB-1, whose black holes were
     later refuted.
4. **Continue / stop.** With S2-like probes, the observable cannot separate a far-side mass from ordinary
   central mass. **Not qualified.**
   - Reopen with a probe at ≲ 100 r_g sampled faster than the far-side period: a pulsar near Sgr A*, or a
     stellar-mass BH–pulsar binary with timing.

## C. Arrival order in strong lensing (a shortcut image arriving before the Fermat minimum)
1. **Unknown.** Does any lensed source show an image that leads the Fermat-potential minimum, or a leading image
   with negative parity (propagation, shortcut)?
2. **Ordinary model.** Smooth lens plus substructure. Substructure is already predicted to cause ordering
   anomalies relative to smooth models (0805.0309; 2405.04779).
3. **Minimum verification.**
   - The theorem itself is settled. In Schneider 1984 (A&A 140, 119), the first image is the global minimum by
     definition, and Σ ≥ 0 is needed only for |μ| ≥ 1.
   - Data: GraL X time-delay compilation CDS J/A+A/707/A345, whose sign convention is undocumented and
     inconsistent. VLBI relative parity exists for B0218+357, PKS 1830-211 and Q0957+561.
   - VLBI gives only relative parity, and the unlensed flux is unknown, so a |μ| ≥ 1 test needs a model.
4. **Continue / stop.** No open unknown that the data can decide beyond the theorem, and substructure mimics an
   ordering anomaly. **Not qualified.**
   - Reopen with a transient source (lensed SN/FRB/GRB) that has model-independent image identification and an
     early, unexplained copy. That overlaps S1/S3's copy searches.
