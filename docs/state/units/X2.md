# Unit X2: Simpson–Visser throat echoes against published post-merger limits (trial unit, D-083)

**Question.** If a binary black hole merger remnant were a symmetric Simpson–Visser (SV) throat with two potential
peaks (2M < a < 3M), would its ringdown echoes exceed the published per-event post-merger strain thresholds?
Which a-range, if any, does the existing analysis already exclude, conditionally on the premises below?
- Bearing on the goal: this tests the **existence** of traversable throats in the only population where many
  remnants are observed. It does not bear on control or usable communication.
- **Ordinary model:** a Kerr black-hole remnant, i.e. ringdown then no post-merger power, plus detector noise.
  Miani et al. (arXiv:2302.12158) give its background as cWB p-values and per-event h_rss thresholds at 50 %
  detection probability and 5 % false-alarm probability (their Table I; a sensitivity figure, not a limit — the
  limits used are Table II's, see S2).

**Premises (unestablished; carried forward):**
- (i) The remnant is a traversable, reflection-symmetric SV throat. SV is an ad hoc metric, and its source and
  stability are unestablished.
- (ii) **No spin.** Real remnants have χ ≈ 0.7. A rotating black-bounce metric exists (Mazza, Franzin & Liberati
  2021), but it is not in this budget. Every result is labelled "non-rotating model".
- (iii) Echo amplitudes come from test-field (s = 0, 1) barriers, as a proxy for gravitational perturbations.
  The true spin-2 perturbation equations depend on the matter that supports SV.
- (iv) Linear propagation from the photon-sphere region.

**Origin.** S0 screen `docs/state/units/S0-2026-10-13.md`, including its addendum. The minimal check that made
A qualify (in `results/x2/s0_echo_delay.json`):
- the echo spacing is Δt = 4 ∫₀^{√(9M²−a²)} dr/f, a power law in ε = 1 − 2M/a;
- Miani et al.'s 50–350 ms window corresponds to a − 2M ≈ 4.5e-4 to 2.2e-2 M for a 60 M☉ remnant.

**Total budget: 16 h**, broken down as follows. Rules: trial-2026-10-13.2.

## Tolerances (tied to the observables)
- **Δt:** 1 %. Miani's windows span a factor of 7, so 1 % is far below the window edges.
- **A:** 10 %. The h_rss thresholds have statistical uncertainties of order 10 %.

## Independence and labels
- Miani et al.'s thresholds are published results, not data we tuned on. The a-ranges are chosen from Δt alone,
  before any amplitude comparison.
- Every bound is conditional: non-rotating SV, test-field proxy, phenomenological model P1–P4, Table II 95 %
  limits. None is a statement about real remnants without those premises.
- No candidate can arise. Miani et al. found no significant post-merger signal.

- **S2, one event (GW150914; derived; `scripts/x2_s2_gw150914.py`, `results/x2/s2_gw150914.json`):**
  - **Inputs:** M_det = 68.8 M☉ and χ = 0.69 (GWTC-1). The ℓ = m = 2 mode is at 249 Hz with τ = 4.1 ms. The
    PMW is [0.2, 1.2] s, and the Table II ratio is 0.21.

    - ringdown-Lorentzian weight: Δt = 0.32–0.58 s, i.e. a − 2M ≈ 2.15e-4 to 7.06e-4 M (2–3 pulses in the PMW);
    - flat weight: only Δt = 0.45–0.58 s (a − 2M ≈ 2.15e-4 to 3.58e-4 M); at 0.32–0.37 s the first two of three
      pulses carry 87 % < 90 % of h_rss²;
    - echo-weighted mean frequency 176–230 Hz; transform-limited duration ≥ 7.4 ms (a lower bound only).
  - **η_max** in the applicable cells, ringdown-Lorentzian / flat 35–350 Hz weight:
    - scalar (s = 0) 0.695–0.728 / 0.987;
    - EM (s = 1) 0.768–0.803 / 1.003;
    - RW-form 1.180–1.235 / 1.010.
  - **Not applicable:**
    - Δt = 0.22–0.27 s: 4–5 pulses in the window, beyond the validated two-pulse morphology;
    - Δt ≥ 0.8 s: only one pulse inside the PMW.
  - **Timing sensitivity (post hoc, from S3):** S3 measures ≈ 48 M (≈ 16 ms for GW150914) of extra delay per
    round trip beyond 4L. Shifting pulse k by k × 16 ms changes the pulse count of no cell except Δt = 0.58 s,
    whose second pulse (1.19 s) then falls inside the edge margin. That cell becomes non-applicable; the η_max
    ranges above do not change (0.45 and 0.52 s give the same values), and the applicable a-range narrows to
    a − 2M ≈ 2.7e-4 to 7.1e-4 M.
  - **Result class:** the outcome falls **between** the two frozen classes. Class 1 (η_max < 1 under all three
    proxies) fails because RW-form gives 1.18–1.24 (and 1.01 flat). Class 2 (η_max ≥ 1 everywhere) fails because
    s = 0 (0.695–0.728 ringdown, 0.987 flat) and s = 1 with the ringdown weight (0.768–0.803) are below 1. So η ~ 1
    is disfavoured only under the scalar and EM proxies (EM with the ringdown weight only), not proxy-independently.
    The one-event limit constrains the inward/outward excitation to order unity: η ≲ 0.7–1.2, depending on proxy
    and weight.
