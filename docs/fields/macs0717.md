# MACS J0717.5+3745 (Hubble Frontier Fields)

Field record for `configs/macs0717.yaml` and the `macs0717-cats` lens model (CATS v4.1 deflection maps, D-033,
D-035). The cluster is at z = 0.545. Every number below is `observed` (images, catalogues), `derived`
(photometry, ratios) or `model_prediction` (images, μ), and every threshold is an ASSUMPTION (D-029, D-031,
D-034, D-035, D-036). No flag is evidence of non-standard lensing.

## Data (accessed 2026-10-08)

- **JWST:** VENUS, program 6882 (PI Fujimoto), NIRCam observation 29, association `t063`, MAST target
  `MACSJ0717+3745`. It was observed 2025-11-02 and released 2025-11-03.
  - **How it was found:** one MAST `query_criteria` call (3′ cone around 109.3864 +37.7550, `NIRCAM/IMAGE`,
    calib_level 3). This is the only public level-3 NIRCam association within 3′ (the query radius). The F200W `s_region`
    contains the cluster centre. The query returned no other program, PEARLS 1176 included.
  - **Bands:** all 10 are PUBLIC: `jw06882-o029_t063_nircam_clear-` + `f090w`, `f115w`, `f150w`, `f200w`,
    `f210m`, `f277w`, `f300m`, `f356w`, `f410m`, `f444w`.
  - **Pipeline:** jwst 3.0.0, photutils 3.0.0 (MAST `prvversion` and the catalogue headers).
  - **Files:** 10 `_cat.ecsv` files (3.4–5.6 MB, 42.3 MB in total) were downloaded. The `_i2d.fits` files
    (0.65 GB LW, 3.1 GB SW) are read only by S3 byte range (`s3://stpubdata/jwst/public/jw06882/L3/t/o029/`).
  - **Records:** `data/manifests/macs0717.ecsv` (10 catalogues with sha256) and
    `data/manifests/macs0717_products.ecsv` (all 20 level-3 products with S3 URIs).
- **DJA:** the grizli v7 index (`https://s3.amazonaws.com/grizli-v2/JwstMosaics/v7/index.html`, 2026-10-08) has
  no MACS0717 mosaic, catalogue or `eazypy.zout`. `curl -I` on `macs0717`, `macsj0717`, `macs0717clu`, `m0717` and
  other names, for v7.0–v7.6, returned 403 (absent).
  - **Consequences:** no matched photometry, no photo-z. The radial screen therefore keeps cluster members and
    foreground sources (`n_not_background_dropped` = 0). The frame offset is measured against the pipeline
    catalogues.
- **Lens model:** CATS v4.1 HLSP maps (deflection, κ, μ(z = 2), `arcs.txt`, `params.txt`), pinned in
  `lensmodel.HFF_CATS` (D-035).
  - **Second models (for D-036):** v4-era HLSP κ/γ maps of Sharon v4cor, GLAFIC v3, Keeton v4, Williams v4.1
    and Diego v4.1. They were read pixel by pixel through HTTP byte ranges and not stored.
  - The Sharon v4cor and Keeton v4 deflection maps (64 MB each, scratch only, not committed) were used to solve
    for images. Only sha256 prefixes were kept (see SOURCES.md).

## Step 4: counter-images and forced photometry (F277W)

- **Prediction:** 51 systems and 199 predicted images. Classes: observed 40, demagnified 2, candidate 68,
  faint 42, no_flux_ref 34, outside 13. The 5σ depth proxy is null for this catalogue.
- **Unpredicted:** 89 of 132 catalogued images have no prediction within the 1.5″ match radius. This follows from
  the model's 3.2″ rms.
- **Forced classes (0.2″ aperture):** recovered 25, confused 38, absent 6, undetectable 13, ambiguous 0,
  no_reference 54, inconsistent_reference 5, off_image 3.
- **Search radius (D-034):** 25 systems at 2.25″ (the "has an unpredicted image" rule), 1 at 1.78″, 1 at 1.03″.
  **0 systems reach the 3″ cap.**
  - The widening is 1.5 × the largest residual of an `observed` image. An image counts as observed only within the
    1.5″ match radius, so the widening can never exceed 2.25″.
  - With rms 3.2″, the model's own copy of a catalogued image usually lands 2–5″ away. It is then searched within
    2.25″ and reported as `absent` or `confused`.
- **Copy test (first pass):** 33 of the 44 `absent` + `confused` rows lie within 5.6″ of a catalogued, unpredicted
  image of the same system, and that image is detected. The per-row verdicts below supersede this count: 31 of those
  rows are counted as copies (absent 3, confused 25); 3 confused rows whose catalogued image is itself undetected
  count as below sensitivity, and the system-65 copies are counted with the 65 pair.
- **Sensitivity run:** `--match-arcsec 3.2` (≈ the model rms), see the end of this note.

## Step 6: vetting of every flag

**Method.** Stamps of 7.4″ in F150W, F277W and F444W (S3 byte ranges, `image_stamper`) of every flagged position
and of each system's catalogued images. All of them were looked at. High-pass versions subtract a 0.6″ median
filter to remove cluster-member light.

**Numbers recorded per flag:**
- 0.2″ aperture S/N at the exact prediction, raw and high-pass, in all three bands;
- high-pass best S/N within the search radius;
- the nearest F200W catalogue source, at the prediction and at the forced best position;
- per reference image: peak and position S/N, peak/position ratio and f(0.2″)/f(0.4″);
- μ at each flag and reference position from the five second models;
- the image sets of Sharon v4cor and Keeton v4 (deflection maps through `MapLensModel`, source at each model's
  own mean back-traced position).

**Noise calibration.** The empirical scatter of 0.2″ aperture S/N at grid offsets in the stamps is 1.4 (F150W,
F277W) and 1.0 (F444W) in units of the scaled ERR. So a pipeline "S/N 5" is about 3.5σ in SW/F277W. No verdict
below depends on a value near 5.

## Summary

- **Screened:** 199 predicted images (144 with forced photometry), 132 images in the flux-ratio test, and 464
  elongated sources giving 11 radial peaks.
- **Flags:** 51 rows, in 26 systems:
  - forced photometry 49 (absent 6, confused 38, inconsistent_reference 5);
  - flux ratio 2;
  - radial 0.
- **Verdicts:**
  - 29 rows: the model's copies of detected catalogued images (rms 3.2″): absent 3, confused 25, and 19's copy of 19.1;
  - 5 rows: untestable μ (near-critical or model-dependent): the 7 pair, 68 (−252), and 75 ×2;
  - 6 rows: CATS-only extra images (D-036 independent-model rule): 3, the 19 extra, 45, 68 ×2, 71;
  - 6 rows: below sensitivity: the 19 central image, 20, 70, and the copies of the undetected 45.3, 79.2, 80.2;
  - 1 row: a counterpart plausible within the position uncertainty (67 third);
  - 4 rows: the 65 pair (two copies and the two flux-ratio flags): a catalogue position offset plus
    model-dependent μ.
  - The `inconsistent_reference` class of system 19 comes from 19.3 being marginally compact.
- **Surviving every ordinary test: 0.** No candidate for `/vet-candidate`, and no anomaly.
- **Limitations:**
  - The CATS v4.1 image list fits this cluster at 3.2″ rms (the release quotes 2.41″), and 47 of 51 systems
    have no redshift in `arcs.txt`; 41 of those take it from `params.txt` `z_m_limit` (D-035). The counter-image test therefore resolves only gross failures, and most raw flags
    are copies of observed images.
  - There is no DJA photo-z, so the radial screen keeps cluster members.
  - VENUS is a single epoch, so there is no time-domain information here.

## Sensitivity: match radius ≈ model rms (`--match-arcsec 3.2`, 118 s, scratch output)

The same `images` command was rerun with `--match-arcsec 3.2` (ASSUMPTION: ≈ the measured rms).
- **Prediction:** observed 102 (was 40); unpredicted catalogued images 25 (was 89).
- **Forced classes:** recovered 5, confused 22, absent 2, undetectable 13, ambiguous 0, no_reference 36,
  inconsistent_reference 2, off_image 2.
- **Search radius:** 15 systems now reach the 3″ cap.
- **Remaining flags:** the two `absent` rows are the near-critical 7 (+50.3) and 68 (−251.9) images. The two
  `inconsistent_reference` rows are 19's CATS-only extra image and its central image. All four are vetted above.
- **Conclusion:** the copy test above accounts for the difference. For a high-rms image list, the forced check
  should use a match radius tied to the measured rms (proposed task).
