# Can a bit A chooses at sending change B's local reading before the external light path? Chandrasekaran (arXiv:2611.34924v1), §4 (2026-11-27)

Owner-requested reading check. Inputs are published equations only; no simulation.

**Source:** V. Chandrasekaran, "An algebraic quantization of gravity from the null cone", arXiv:2611.34924v1 (submitted
2026-11-25). The arXiv LaTeX source has sha256 1251f05ae2fac0bc307286550e115f3a9ef9228187822b9f40aad9c33b3407be.
Equation numbers were read from the arXiv HTML rendering of v1.

**Labels:** [lit] the paper's text; [der] a step derived here from it; [missing] needed but not supplied.

**Control taken as given (owner):** in the two-sided state (5.75), a local phase flip at A leaves B's one-sided
distribution unchanged. §2 shows that the same holds for every A-side boundary source.

**Answer** (details in §1–§3):
- The only action that carries A's post-sending choice to B is A's own source operator S_I(F_s) (4.1). Its effect
  reaches B through the reduced-Hamiltonian evolution composed across cuts (4.29); at one loop that effect is the
  retarded term in (6.16).
- B can distinguish the two bits no earlier than the moment the future light cone of A's sending event reaches B's
  detector. In the two-boundary state (5.75) B can never distinguish them.
- So the answer is **no**: nothing arrives earlier than the external light path between the same endpoints.

## 1. Item by item

**Common set-up for both sendings** [lit]:
- incoming Gaussian state ρ_G on Σ₀ ((4.7); §6.2);
- the corner area sector and normal frame at S;
- the clock normalisation at ∂Σ₀ (3.28);
- B's detector, prepared before A chooses.

Only the deformation F_s differs between the two sendings. It is switched on by A at its sending time t_s and
supported in A's region.

**B's clock:**
- in a causal diamond: B's own affine parameter along its generator from S, or the gravitational clock û_* (§4);
- in the two-boundary state: proper time at B's wall, whose translations are generated by Ĥ (§5.4).

**External light path:** the null geodesic from A's sending event to B's detector through the joined region.

**Why the relative boost leaves B's one-sided statistics alone [der].**
- It acts by e^{iηŝ/2π} on area eigenstates.
- Because s_L = s_R, the area is a single shared corner variable. A B-local operator cannot change s_R without also
  changing s_L.
- So B-local observables commute with ŝ, and their one-sided distributions are unchanged.

## 2. Minimal case where the paper's dynamics suffice: the two-boundary state (5.75)
- **Inputs:** (5.75) gives |Ψ_ω⟩ ∝ Σₙ e^{−nbω}|n⟩_L|n*⟩_R, with b = ħβ_R/2 [lit]. Let A apply any unitary source U_s ⊗ 1 at
  its wall; by (5.72) and (5.74) this acts on A's factor only.
- **Result** [der]: B's reduced state, and hence the probability of reading n gravitons in mode ω, is the same for both
  bits:

  P(n | s = 0) = P(n | s = 1) = (1 − e^{−2bω}) e^{−2nbω}.

- **Consistency check** [der]: the same state gives ⟨a_L a_R⟩ = 1/(2 sinh bω), as printed after (5.75).
- **Errors:**
  - The equality uses only the factorisation (5.74) and the one-sided generation of sources (5.72). It does not depend
    on the second-order expansion of H_c (r_h ≫ ℓ_P, one odd-parity quadrupole) used to write (5.75).
  - Truncating the sum at n ≤ N changes the normalisation by at most e^{−2(N+1)bω}, equally for both bits.

## 3. Operators whose definition spans both places, and what implementing them needs
- **Corner and frame operations** ((D.13), §3.6, App. C.1):
  - They act on data that both regions share at S: the area, the relative boost and the edge modes.
  - Implementing one needs physical access to S.
  - Comparing frames across S needs "a null congruence across it" (§5.2) [lit]. B therefore learns the result no
    earlier than light from S.
- **A deformation nonlocal across the spatial boundary** (§4.1, text after (4.1): "Its dependence on fields across a
  spatial boundary may be nonlocal") [missing].
  - This is the one operator that could make B's later one-sided reading depend on A's bit without propagation.
  - The paper defines such F formally but gives none that A alone can switch, and no dynamics that realises one.
  - Implementing it needs a physical coupling between the two regions: an interaction carried by fields crossing the
    region between them, or classical communication plus local operations. Both use the external path.

## 4. Not claimed
- No channel, no bit rate, and no superluminal effect. The two-boundary case has no exterior path, so no comparison of
  arrival times is defined there.
- No statement beyond one loop for (6.16), or beyond the quadratic order of (5.75).
