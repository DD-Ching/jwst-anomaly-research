# Counter-images of uncatalogued bright arcs (plan, 2026-10-14)

Hypothesis: every strongly magnified background arc in `results/arcs/*/arcs.ecsv` that is not a catalogued
multiple image has the counter-images its lens model predicts at its photo-z (z16, z50, z84). A predicted
counter-image brighter than 10 sigma that is absent in forced photometry at every photo-z would be a lens-model
residual. Ordinary explanations: photo-z error (wrong z moves or removes the counter-image), the arc is a cluster
member or foreground, the counter-image sits on a cluster galaxy/ICL, model resolution at folds (D-045).
