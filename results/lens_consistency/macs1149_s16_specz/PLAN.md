# MACS1149 system 16: published spectroscopic redshift check (plan, before any catalogue is read)

D-037 "Revisit if": a spectroscopic z for system 16 would fix the fitted-redshift question (CATS fits z 4.419; both
CATS and Sharon v4cor move the flagged extra image onto 16.2 at z 2.5–3.0; CANUCS DR1 photo-z of 16.2 is 2.25,
95 % 0.23–2.33).

- **Hypothesis under test:** the flagged extra image of system 16 is a model artefact of a wrong fitted redshift.
- **Ordinary explanations:** wrong z (D-037), pair topology, wrong cross-identification of 16.1 / 16.2.
- **Data:** published MUSE / spectroscopic catalogues of MACS1149 on VizieR (Grillo et al. 2016; Treu et al. 2016
  compilation), matched by position to CATS v4.1 `arcs.txt` 16.1 / 16.2 within 0.5″ (ASSUMPTION).
- **Outcomes (frozen):**
  - spec-z for 16.1 or 16.2 with z ≤ 3.5 → "fitted z wrong": the extra-image flag is explained (D-037 addendum).
  - spec-z near 4.42 (|Δz| ≤ 0.1) → "fitted z confirmed": the flag reopens as testable; re-run forced photometry
    for the predicted image at that z.
  - no spec-z → "not decided"; D-037 unchanged.
