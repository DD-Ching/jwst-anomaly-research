## Reading and limits
- No two-band ≥ 0.3 mag change at ≥ 5σ among the catalogued positions of the overlap over 37 d other than
  star-spike and edge groups. Together with the 392.6 d pair (#571) this is a two-baseline null in the MACS J1931
  core; it is not a lens-model test. A caustic-crossing star that brightens by ≥ 0.75 mag in ≥ 2 LW bands at a
  catalogued position would have been recovered with probability 0.94 (automatic thresholds only).
- Only catalogued positions are measured; LW bands only; recovery is by the automatic thresholds, not emulated
  vetting. The stars' spikes cover a few arcsec² around each of the five brightest stars, where the test is blind.
- `grid.ecsv` is not kept, as on the earlier grids; it is regenerated by the command in `PLAN.md` with
  `--epoch2-catalogs 3 4 5` (about 15 s once the mosaics are local).

  run's counts (14 hits, 7 groups, G1 0.916) were printed. This result is the corrected run.
