## Post hoc (not in the plan; `posthoc.json`)
- **Detector level** (`detector_level`; the twelve 12299 o001 NRCBLONG `_cal` exposures, header SIP WCS, pixels read by
  S3 byte range): groups 5, 7, 8, 9 (`g03575`), 10 and 11 each map to one detector pixel, identical in the F277W,
  F356W and F444W exposure of the same dither (post hoc; e.g. group 7: (1982, 972) in all three), with DQ 4 (JUMP_DET) in every
  filter and, where the 5 × 5 stamp is finite, peak fraction 0.94–1.02 (a single pixel). A sky source would not stay
  on one pixel through a 3-filter sequence and would be PSF-broadened; a pixel that jumps in every exposure is a
  detector artefact that a single exposure cannot reject. `g03578` (group 9) lies within 10 px of the detector edge
  and is not measured there; group 5's stamp is not finite, so only its pixel and DQ are reported.
- **Mosaic level** (`mosaic_level`): every hit of groups 4, 5 and 7–11 lies where one 12299 exposure contributes
  (`CON`); groups 0–3 and 6 have 2–4. Post hoc, groups 5 and 7–11 have peak fractions 0.34–0.70 (F277W) and 0.38–0.65 (F356W)
  against post hoc reference maxima 0.222 / 0.177 (195 / 194 bright catalogued sources, hits excluded).
- 12299's F356W / F444W exposures (601 s / 515 s, `epoch_inventory/macs1931_epochs.json`) are shallower than its
  F277W (2104.4 s); F277W still has the lowest per-band recovery (0.818) and the largest noise scale.

## Reading and limits
- No two-band ≥ 0.3 mag change among the catalogued positions of the overlap over 392.6 d other than SN Eos and
  instrumental groups. This is a two-epoch null in the MACS J1931 core; it is not a lens-model test.
- Only catalogued positions are measured (101.1 has no position of its own); LW bands only; recovery is by the
  automatic thresholds, not emulated vetting. Single-exposure regions of 12299 keep detector artefacts: a hit there
  needs the detector-level check above before it is believed.
