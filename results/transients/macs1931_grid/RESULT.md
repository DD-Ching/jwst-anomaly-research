## Reading and limits
- No two-band ≥ 0.3 mag change among the catalogued positions of the overlap over 392.6 d other than SN Eos and
  instrumental groups. This is a two-epoch null in the MACS J1931 core; it is not a lens-model test.
- Only catalogued positions are measured (101.1 shows this blind spot); LW bands only; recovery is by the automatic
  thresholds, not emulated vetting. Single-exposure regions of 12299 carry unrejected cosmic rays: a transient
  there would need a second exposure or band-to-band shape checks to be believed.
- `grid.ecsv` (0.75 MB) is not kept, as on the earlier grids; it is regenerated by the command in `PLAN.md` in about
  15 s once the mosaics are local.
