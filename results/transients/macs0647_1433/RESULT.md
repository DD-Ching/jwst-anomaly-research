# MACS0647 1433 o010 → o020: F200W two-epoch grid and core difference pass (result, 2026-10-26)

Plan `PLAN.md` (pushed before any pixel was read; one amendment before any pixel, the core box size). Tools
`scripts/transient_grid.py` and `scripts/arc_diff.py`, unchanged. Imaging (observed): program 1433 NIRCam F200W
level-3 `_i2d`, o010 (2022-09-23) and o020 (2023-01-08), 107 d, aperture position angles 280.8° and 170.4° (a 110°
roll; `g85_vet.json`). Numbers: `grid/summary.json`, `grid/hits.ecsv`, `core/summary.json`, `core/hits.ecsv.gz`,
`g85_vet.json`, `g85_hst.json`. Every number is derived; thresholds are the D-027 ASSUMPTIONs.

## Frozen result
- **Run 1 (catalogue grid): undetermined (U).** G1 fails: −0.75 mag injections recovered 0.802 (< 0.85; 0.840 where
  the fraction set the flux); −0.44 mag 0.27. G2 passes (noise scale 1.47). 5,082 positions, 1,731 measured (the
  rotated footprints overlap only in part), 224 hits in 95 groups.
- **Run 2 (difference pass, ±55″ box on the multiple-image region): G3 passes** (27.5 AB injections 0.94; 0.73 away
  from bright cores), noise scale 1.22, but **9,154 hits** (7,592 not near a bright core) in one band: too many to
  vet by eye, so run 2 gives no null.
- **Outcome C:** one hit group, **g85** (run 2 `h1023`, 39σ, 0.03″ from the run 1 group), has no instrumental
  explanation (below) → `/vet-candidate`, `needs-human`. It is not on a catalogued multiple image: the nearest
  Meena et al. 2023 image is 2.1 at 6.0″ (not stored). No run 1 hit lies within 0.5″ of a Meena image; 48 of the 72
  images have a measured grid position (not stored).

## Contact sheets (looked at; post hoc classification by eye, not stored as a table)
- All 95 run 1 groups at low resolution, 22 of them zoomed. Most are diffraction spikes and star cores under the
  110° roll, mosaic edges, and o020 scattered-light streaks. Five zoomed non-spike groups sit on pixels with fewer
  input exposures (CON map): g32 (o010, 2 exposures, a sharp saturated block: cosmic ray), g31 and g60 (diffuse
  blobs at 2 exposures), g58 (a ring at 1 exposure: snowball or cosmic ray), g62 (an o020 wisp).
- **g85** (RA 101.99665, Dec +70.250721): a point source in o020 on a crowded field of faint galaxies, absent in
  o010 (`g85_sheet.png`).

## g85 vetting (post hoc; `docs/candidates/macs0647-1433-g85.md`)
- Seen in three o020 `_cal` exposures, on two detectors and at different pixels (peak S/N 10.2, 9.9, 9.3; ≤ 1 px from
  the predicted position); the fourth falls off the detector. Not a cosmic ray, hot pixel or persistence; no
  motion within the 30 min visit (≤ 1 px of 0.031″), so not a Solar System object.
- Post hoc: aperture (0.15″) difference flux 112 nJy, 26.28 AB without aperture correction; o010 4 exposures, o020 3.
  Same-visit F480M: peak S/N 1.2 (not detected).
- Post hoc: HST F110W PID 17504 minus CLASH 2011 (2023-12-09 to 2024-03-21): no detection in the five registered visits
  (S/N −1.7 to +1.0; aperture 5σ 25.41–26.36 AB); `ifaa27010` fails registration (3.6″).
- Post hoc: NED (5″): two catalogued galaxies, [CDK2017] 07064 at 0.02′ and [CDK2017] 07127 at 0.082′, no redshift. SIMBAD 5″
  and Gaia 3″: none.
- **Verdict: inconclusive: needs a TNS check and the host redshift.** A real transient that appeared between
  2022-09-23 and 2023-01-08 and was not seen at the HST limits 11–14 months later. The ordinary reading, a
  supernova in a field galaxy or cluster member, is a `hypothesis`; it is not tested here.
